Running late again? Shoo is the alarm that knows where you're going.

Tell Shoo where you need to be and when: a work shift, class, the gym, the dentist, a flight, dinner. It works out exactly when you have to walk out the door, checks the drive, and starts nudging you before it's time. The longer you sit there, the less polite it gets.

👟 GENTLE: "Hey, you might wanna start putting your shoes on."
🚪 GETTING SERIOUS: "Shoo. Shoo. SHOO."
🚨 PANIC MODE: "...This is me not asking nicely anymore. LEAVE."
🏆 MADE IT: "Look at you, on time like a responsible adult."

The moment you head out, Shoo notices and backs off. No more snoozing a generic alarm and losing track of time.

WHY SHOO
• Real leave times. Shoo works back from the start time: arrive-early buffer, travel time with traffic, and the minutes you need to get out the door.
• It notices you left. Set location to "Always" and Shoo stops nagging the second you're on your way, even when the app is closed.
• Jokes that fit the plan. Different lines for work, school, the gym, the doctor, a flight, a date, a job interview and more.
• Your schedule, wherever it lives. Add plans by hand, repeat them weekly, read your phone's calendar or iPhone Reminders, link Google, Outlook or iCloud calendars, or pull shifts from Homebase, When I Work, 7shifts and Deputy.
• Quick add. Paste a schedule or a confirmation email, or pick a screenshot of your shifts. Screenshots are read on your phone, never uploaded.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Your plans and places stay on your phone.

Built for shift workers, students, and anyone whose brain says "I have plenty of time" right up until they don't.
