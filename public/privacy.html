Shoo privacy policy

Shoo: Never Be Late. · Last updated November 3, 2026

The short version: Shoo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your plans, places and settings stay on your phone. Shoo only sends what it has to in order to look up travel times and read calendars you link, and nobody keeps it.

What stays on your phone

Your saved places, plans, settings, notification history, and anything Shoo reads from your phone's calendar or Reminders are stored only on your device. Screenshots you pick for Quick add are read on the device; the picture is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your phone, and why

WhatSent toWhy
Your current location and the place you're going (coordinates) Shoo's server, which asks Google Maps; or directly to OpenStreetMap's routing service (OSRM) if the server can't be reached To work out how long the trip takes, with traffic
Addresses and place names you search for Shoo's server, which asks Google Maps; or OpenStreetMap's Nominatim To find the place on the map
Calendar links you paste in (iCal addresses) Shoo's server, or the calendar's own website To read your upcoming events
Purchases of Shoo Pro Apple or Google, and RevenueCat To unlock Pro and restore it on a new phone

None of these requests carry your name, email, phone number or any account. Shoo's server does not store them; it answers and forgets. It keeps a short-lived count of requests per network address to stop abuse. Google, OpenStreetMap and RevenueCat handle the data under their own privacy policies: Google, OpenStreetMap Foundation, RevenueCat.

Location in the background

If you set location to "Always", Shoo collects your location to notice when you leave and stop nagging you, and to notice when you arrive, even when the app is closed or not in use. It does this with geofences: your phone tells Shoo when you cross the edge of the area you're leaving or the place you're headed. That location is used only on your phone and is not sent anywhere. You can switch it off any time in your phone's settings.

Calendar and Reminders

If you let Shoo read your phone's calendar or Reminders, it reads events and reminders that have a time, only to find where you need to be and when. It never changes them, and they stay on your phone. If an event has an address, that address is looked up on the map as described above.

Notifications

Shoo's nags are local notifications scheduled on your phone. There are no push servers.

Sharing and selling

Shoo does not sell your data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it to track you across other apps and websites.

Children

Shoo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can deny or change location, calendar, Reminders and notification permissions at any time in your phone's settings; Shoo still works with a typed-in starting point and the "I've left" button. Because Shoo has no accounts and keeps nothing about you on a server, there is nothing to delete beyond uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
