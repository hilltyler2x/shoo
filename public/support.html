Shoo support

Shoo: Never Be Late. Knows when you need to leave, and nags you until you do.

Get help

Open an issue at github.com/hilltyler2x/shoo/issues and describe what happened, which phone you use, and the time of the plan. We read every one.

Shoo didn't notice I left

Set Shoo's location permission to Always (iPhone: Settings → Shoo → Location → Always; Android: Settings → Apps → Shoo → Permissions → Location → Allow all the time). If you swipe Shoo away on iPhone it can't wake up, so leave it in the background. You can always tap "I've left" on any nag.

I'm not getting nags

Check that notifications are on for Shoo, and on Android that "Alarms & reminders" is allowed for Shoo. Open Shoo once after restarting your phone.

Restore Shoo Pro

Open Setup → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with. To cancel, use your phone's subscriptions page (App Store → your name → Subscriptions, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions).

Privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
